Privacy.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Picnote keeps your lists and notes, for yourself or shared with one other person. We only handle what that takes: no ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

Who is responsible

Picnote is published by Yoann Picquenot and Mel J, as individuals. They are the controllers of your data. Questions: hello@picnote.cc.

The data Picnote handles

Data Why
Your email address Signing you in with a code sent by email. No password.
Your first name and the color you pick Showing the other member of your household what you added, checked or changed.
Your lists, tasks and notes The service itself: keeping them and syncing them across your devices.
Your household and invitations Sharing lists and notes with another person. An invitation keeps the invited person’s email address and a code.
The history of changes Showing “Latest changes” and what each of you changed last: who, what, when, and the title of the task concerned.
Your presence and live typing Showing the other member of your household that you have the app open, and what you type as you type it. This is passed on live and not stored.
A notification token and the app’s language Sending you the reminders and notifications you turned on (for example when the other member creates or changes something), in your language.
Your notification settings Following your choices: notifications on or off, reminder time.

This processing is necessary to provide the service you ask for by using Picnote (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Picnote uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Who sees your data

  • You. Your personal lists and notes are visible to you only.
  • The other member of your household, if you have one: they see the lists and notes you share, your first name, your color, your presence and the history of changes to what is shared.
  • Our technical providers, who host and carry the data on our behalf and may not use it for themselves:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and sign-in emails. The data is hosted in London (United Kingdom), a country the European Commission recognizes as providing an adequate level of protection.
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications to your phone. The content of a notification (for example a list’s name) goes through Google to be delivered. This transfer is covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

How long

  • Your data is kept as long as your account exists.
  • Checked tasks leave your lists the next day, but stay archived in your account until it is deleted.
  • An invitation is kept until it is accepted, sent again, or the household is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → My account → Delete my account. You have 15 days to change your mind. After that, your account, profile, personal lists and notes and settings are erased for good.

Shared lists and notes stay with the other member of the household, without your name. If you were alone in your household, they are erased with it. You can also ask for deletion by email at hello@picnote.cc.

Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it, get a copy in a readable format (portability), object to its processing or ask for it to be restricted. Write to hello@picnote.cc: we answer within one month.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or to the authority of the country where you live.

Minimum age

Picnote is for people aged 15 and over. If you are under 15, you need a parent’s consent.

Security

Everything between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Each person can only reach their own data and their household’s, which the database checks on every request.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we will let you know in the app. The date at the top of this page shows the latest update.
